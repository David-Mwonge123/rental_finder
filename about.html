About Nairobi Rentals

Nairobi Rentals helps students and young professionals find verified houses in Nairobi without agents or hidden fees. Every listing shows the real price, water, wifi, security and the landlord's direct phone number. No middlemen, no fake listings.

How It Works

  1. 1. Search: Filter by area, type and price on Listings.
  2. 2. Compare & Save: Tap ♥ to save, or compare 2 houses side-by-side.
  3. 3. Contact: Call or WhatsApp the landlord directly from the Details page.
  4. 4. Book: Book a viewing and manage it in My Bookings.

Help / FAQ

Is it free? Yes. Searching is free. Landlords list for free.

How do I book a viewing? Open a house -> Book Viewing -> pick date -> see it in My Bookings.

How do I delete my listing? Go to My Listings -> Delete.

Why is my listing not showing? Refresh Listings. Your listings are saved on this device only.
